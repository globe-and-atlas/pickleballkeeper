# Scoring formats reviewed on 2026-10-24

- **Standard side-out:** only the serving side scores. Doubles opens at 0–0–2; after that opening fault, service changes sides. Subsequent service turns get server 1 then server 2. Singles has one server. Games are commonly to 11, win by two; 15 and 21 appear in tournament formats. [USA Pickleball rules summary](https://usapickleball.org/rules/summary/) and [USA Pickleball instructions](https://usapickleball.org/pickleball-skills/level-one/how-to-play-pickleball/).
- **Doubles service court:** the first serve after every side-out starts from the right court, even if the serving team's score is odd. The same server alternates courts after scoring, and server 2 starts from the opposite court after server 1's fault. Do not derive the side-out doubles serve court from total score parity. [USA Pickleball rules summary](https://usapickleball.org/rules/summary/).
- **PPA:** pro games to 11, win by two, side-out; most matches best of three, major finals best of five. [PPA explanation](https://www.ppatour.com/about/what-is-pickleball/).
- **MLP 2026:** doubles use side-out to 11; the DreamBreaker tiebreak uses rally to 21 with a four-point player rotation. An ordinary single-match scorer can score a doubles game, but cannot manage the entire team event or player rotation. [MLP format](https://majorleaguepickleball.co/abcs-of-mlp/).
- **USA Pickleball 2026 rally option:** point on every rally, including the game-winning point when receiving; this changed from 2025. [Approved change](https://rules.usapickleball.org/view/rule-changes-view-all/entry/2378/?pagenum=1) and [2026 official rulebook page](https://usapickleball.org/rules/).
- **CPT 2025–26:** individual games use rally to 21, win by two, with win-on-serve freeze only when the receiving side is at game point and leading. A team match has four doubles games and, if tied, a DreamBreaker. [CPT rules](https://www.dupr.com/rules/collegiate-pickleball-rules).
- **DUPR high school 2025–26:** the rule page describes a 21-point rally format with freeze, but its tournament-flow section separately says pool games to 11 and elimination games to 15. Event rules and director choice control. Do not claim a universal high-school preset. [DUPR high-school rules](https://www.dupr.com/rules/high-school-pickleball-rules).
